Bluetooth and Location

The Application's core feature is finding nearby Bluetooth devices (such as AirPods and earbuds) using your device's Bluetooth radio. Bluetooth scanning happens entirely on your device; the Application does not transmit the Bluetooth devices it detects to any server.

If you grant permission, the Application uses your device's location while scanning so that it can remember roughly where a saved device was last seen. This location data is stored only on your device (locally, using standard iOS on-device storage) and is never uploaded, transmitted, or sold to any server or third party. You can revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings, or by uninstalling the Application.
