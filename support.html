Common Questions

  • The app can't find my device. Make sure Bluetooth is turned on for both your phone and the device you're looking for, and that the device is powered on and within range.
  • Signal strength seems inaccurate. Bluetooth signal strength (RSSI) is affected by walls, distance, and interference from other devices — treat it as an approximate guide rather than an exact distance.
  • I want to cancel my subscription. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID — open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone to manage or cancel.

We appreciate your feedback and take all messages into consideration as we work to improve the app.
